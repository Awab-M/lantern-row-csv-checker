What gets checked

Focused where mistakes hide.

  • Broken row structure
  • Empty or repeated headers
  • Missing handles or titles
  • Duplicate nonblank SKUs
  • Price and compare-at formatting
  • Unusual image URLs

Findings are indicators, not a guarantee of import compatibility. Shopify variants may legitimately share handles; supplier exports and other platforms can use different columns.
